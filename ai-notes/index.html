Part 2: The Deep Learning & Generative AI Era

Following the early milestones, AI evolved from rules-based engines to statistical pattern recognition and large language models.

2006 - Deep Learning
Neural Net Revival
Geoffrey Hinton & team popularized Deep Belief Networks & Backpropagation training for deep architectures.
2012 - AlexNet
GPU Computer Vision
Alex Krizhevsky used NVIDIA GPUs for ImageNet competition — reducing error rates from 26% to 15%.
2017 - Transformers
Attention Is All You Need
Google introduced the Transformer architecture, replacing RNNs/LSTMs with Self-Attention mechanisms.
2022+ - Generative AI
LLMs & Diffusion
ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini & Midjourney bring AGI-level natural language reasoning and creative generation to millions.
“ Scaling Laws showed that adding parameters, data, and compute predictably increases model reasoning capability.

Analysis: Are Machines Smarter Than Humans?

The answer depends on whether we measure Narrow Specialization vs General Intelligence (AGI).

Dimension Human Brain 🧠 AI Systems 🤖
Processing Speed ~100 Hz signal transmission GigaFLOPS / TeraFLOPS parallel compute
Memory Capacity Selective, associative memory Petabytes of lossless retrieval
Specialized Tasks (Chess/Math/Code) Human champions surpassed Superhuman performance (DeepBlue, AlphaGo)
Generalization & Empathy Unmatched emotional, physical & common-sense reasoning Hallucinations, lacks true biological experience
Conclusion: Machines excel at specialized computation & scale; humans excel at consciousness, empathy & novel reasoning!
